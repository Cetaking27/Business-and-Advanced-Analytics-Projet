# Business and Advanced Analytics Projects

A collection of projects completed during our Business and Advanced Analytics studies at **NBCC (New Brunswick Community College)**, covering data cleaning, statistical analysis, SQL, Python, R, machine learning, dashboards, and business insights.

## Purpose

This repository documents our learning and practical application of analytics to business problems. It will grow as we complete new assignments and projects throughout the program.

## Topics Covered

- Data cleaning, preparation, and validation
- Exploratory data analysis and applied statistics
- SQL databases and data engineering
- Python and R programming
- Machine learning and predictive analytics
- Data visualization and dashboards
- Business requirements, reporting, and decision-making

## Projects

### Customer Churn Analysis

Explore customer characteristics and churn patterns using descriptive statistics, frequency distributions, and contingency tables. Examine associations between churn, contract type, payment method, and location without claiming causation from exploratory results.

Additional projects will be added as the program progresses. Each project will include its own objectives, methods, and findings.
